Decentralized AI Architecture

Federated Learning: Collaborative AI Without Data Sharing

Train high-performance machine learning models across decentralized edge devices or silos holding local data samples, without ever exchanging raw datasets. Preserving privacy by design.

Privacy by Design

Raw user data never leaves the local device or secure server silo. Only encrypted model updates (gradients/weights) are transmitted.

Distributed Computation

Leverage idle compute power across millions of edge devices, smartphones, or hospitals to train models collaboratively.

Non-IID Robustness

Advanced algorithms like FedProx and FedDyn tackle statistical heterogeneity where local data distributions vary wildly across nodes.

Centralized vs. Federated Learning

Understanding the paradigm shift in machine learning infrastructure.

Traditional Centralized Training
  • Requires centralizing all raw data in one database/cloud.
  • High regulatory risk (GDPR, HIPAA compliance hurdles).
  • Vulnerable to single-point data breaches and leaks.
Federated Learning Paradigm
  • Data remains stationary on local client devices or nodes.
  • Only parameterized model updates (weights) are shared.
  • Compliant with strict data privacy laws natively.

Federated Optimization Algorithms

Comparing foundational mathematical formulations from landmark research literature.

FedSGD

Communication Heavy

Clients compute gradients over their local datasets using a single batch and send them to the central server for averaging.

w_{t+1} = w_t - \eta \sum_{k=1K} \frac{n_k}{n} g_k
Key Paper: McMahan et al., 2016
Limitation: High communication cost per update iteration.
GOLD STANDARD

FedAvg (Federated Averaging)

Multi-step SGD

Allows clients to perform multiple local epochs of SGD before sending updated weights to the aggregator, drastically reducing communication rounds.

w_{t+1} = \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{n_k}{n} w_t^{k}
Key Paper: Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data (AISTATS 2017)
Advantage: Fast convergence on IID and moderately non-IID data.

FedProx

Non-IID Robust

Adds a proximal term to the local objective function to restrict local updates from drifting too far from the global model, handling severe statistical heterogeneity.

h_k(w; w_t) = F_k(w) + \frac{\mu}{2} ||w - w_t||^2
Key Paper: Li et al., 2018 (MLSys 2020)
Advantage: Guarantees convergence on highly skewed non-IID data.

FedDyn

Dynamic Regularization

Employs dynamic regularizers matching local objectives to the global objective, eliminating client drift and aligning stationary points.

F_k^{dyn}(w; w_t, \alpha) = F_k(w) - \langle \nabla F_k(w_t) - \alpha h_t, w \rangle + \frac{\alpha}{2} ||w||^2
Key Paper: Acar et al., 2021 (ICLR)
Advantage: Asymptotic exactness for heterogeneous objectives.

Security, Cryptography & Privacy Protections

Defending decentralized networks against membership inference, model poisoning, and data reconstruction attacks.

Differential Privacy (DP)

Injecting calibrated Gaussian or Laplacian noise directly into local model updates before transmission, ensuring individual training samples cannot be reverse-engineered.

Guarantee: $(\epsilon, \delta)$-indistinguishability.

Secure Aggregation (SecAgg)

Uses cryptographic secret sharing protocols so that the central server can compute the sum of client updates without ever inspecting individual client vectors.

Protocol: Bonawitz et al. (CCS 2017)

Byzantine Defenses

Defending against malicious clients injecting poisoned model weights to sabotage global accuracy. Employs Krum, Trimmed Mean, and coordinate-wise median aggregation.

Defense: Robust statistical filtering.

Real-World Domain Applications

Where federated learning solves critical data privacy and regulatory barriers.

Healthcare & Genomics

Hospitals train diagnostic models on sensitive patient EHRs and MRI scans collaboratively without transferring records across institutional boundaries, complying fully with HIPAA and GDPR.

Banking & Fraud Detection

Multi-institutional fraud detection networks where international banks pool pattern recognition capabilities without sharing proprietary transaction records or customer PII.

Edge Mobile & IoT Fleet

Smartphones and autonomous vehicles improve next-word prediction models and edge perception locally while keeping personal messaging logs and sensor feeds private.

Seminal Research Literature

Core academic papers that shaped the field of Federated Learning.

AISTATS 2017 McMahan et al. (Google)

Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data

The foundational paper introducing Federated Averaging (FedAvg), demonstrating that deep neural networks can be trained over decentralized data silos using iterative model weight averaging.

MLSys 2020 Li et al. (Carnegie Mellon University)

Federated Optimization in Heterogeneous Networks (FedProx)

Addresses statistical and systems heterogeneity across edge devices by introducing a proximal term to stabilize local training updates on non-IID data.

Read arXiv
CCS 2017 Bonawitz et al. (Google)

Practical Secure Aggregation for Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

Proposes a cryptographic secure aggregation protocol allowing the server to compute the sum of high-dimensional client gradient vectors securely without inspecting individual updates.
